---
title: Accessible by default
description: Why landmarks, focus behavior, and readable metadata belong in the foundation rather than a final pass.
publishedAt: 2026-01-30
draft: false
---

Accessibility is most dependable when it is part of the page structure.
Semantic landmarks and visible focus states then benefit every route
automatically.

Automated checks do not replace thoughtful review, but they protect the
baseline while the site grows and changes hands.
